About this guide

Purpose

Many travelers know the big names of Mindanao but not the places in Davao del Norte. This website gathers beginner-friendly information about the province in one organized place.

About the province

Davao del Norte is a province in the Davao Region of Mindanao. Its capital is Tagum City. The province includes Panabo City, known as the Banana Capital of the Philippines, and the Island Garden City of Samal in Davao Gulf.

Intended users

  • First-time visitors who need a simple starting point
  • Students and families planning short trips
  • Local residents who want to discover nearby places

Project note

This is a student project for IT111 at Davao del Norte State College. Confirm schedules, fees, and travel advisories with the local tourism office before you go.
